4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Lessee has deposited with the Lessor a sum of INR 70,000/- (equivalent to two months' rent) as interest-free refundable security deposit.
The Lessor covenants to refund the full security deposit within 14 (fourteen) business days following peaceful handover of the vacant possession, subject only to deductions for unpaid electricity/water bills and documented structural repairs exceeding ordinary wear and tear.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
The Lessor shall be solely responsible for all major and structural repairs (including seepage, main electrical wiring, plumbing mains, and roof integrity). The Lessee shall be responsible for routine minor repairs (such as fuse replacement and tap washers) not exceeding INR 1,500 per incident.

6. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 1 (one) month's prior written notice or by paying 1 (one) month's rent in lieu thereof. Neither party shall be subject to any lock-in penalty beyond the initial 3 months.

7. INSPECTION AND PRIVACY
The Lessor or his authorized agent shall have the right to inspect the Demised Premises only after giving at least 24 hours prior written notice to the Lessee and during daylight hours (10:00 AM to 6:00 PM), respecting the reasonable privacy of the Lessee.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India and the jurisdiction of competent civil courts in New Delhi. In the event of any dispute arising out of this Agreement, parties agree to first explore amicable mediation through the Delhi State Legal Services Authority (DSLSA) or an accredited mediator before initiating formal litigation.
